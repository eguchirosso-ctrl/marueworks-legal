Support

If something is not working, or you have a question about N3 Bridge, this page explains what to check first and where to write.

App issues

If the app closes unexpectedly, a screen stays empty, or the Japanese pronunciation does not play, first close the app completely and open it again, then check that your device is running the latest version of the app and of iOS.

If the problem stays, please write and tell us what you were doing when it happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version.

Purchase and restore

Premium is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, so it can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling the app.

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

If Premium still does not appear after restoring, please write and include the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.

Learning content

If you find a word, reading, meaning, or example sentence that looks wrong, please tell us. Include the Japanese word and what you would expect instead.

Reports about the study content are read and used to correct the vocabulary in a later update.

Privacy questions

The Privacy Policy in this app explains what information is handled and which third-party services are involved.

If something there is unclear, or you want to ask about the information held about you, please write to the same address below.

Contact

Please write in English or Japanese. Replies may take a few days.
