Privacy Policy for N3 Bridge

Last updated: September 8, 2026

N3 Bridge is a Japanese vocabulary learning application provided by MARUE WORKS.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use N3 Bridge.

1. Information We Collect

N3 Bridge does not require you to create an account.

Your learning progress, including vocabulary study status and review progress, is stored locally on your device.

N3 Bridge does not operate its own server for storing your learning data.

2. In-App Purchases

N3 Bridge offers an optional premium purchase.

Purchase processing and purchase status management are handled through Apple’s App Store and RevenueCat.

N3 Bridge may receive purchase-related information necessary to determine whether premium features are available.

N3 Bridge does not receive or store your full payment card information.

For more information about how Apple and RevenueCat handle data, please review their respective privacy policies.

3. Advertising

The free version of N3 Bridge may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Advertising services may process information such as device information, advertising-related identifiers, IP address, and interaction information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

N3 Bridge does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not intentionally use tracking across other companies’ apps or websites for personalized advertising.

4. Advertising Privacy Choices

Where applicable, advertising privacy choices may be provided through Google’s consent and privacy management features.

The availability of these controls may depend on your country or region.

5. Microphone and Speech

N3 Bridge includes Japanese pronunciation playback.

This feature uses text-to-speech functionality for Japanese vocabulary readings.

N3 Bridge does not use the microphone and does not record your voice.

N3 Bridge does not use speech recognition.

6. Data Sharing

N3 Bridge does not sell your personal information.

N3 Bridge does not maintain its own user account database and does not intentionally upload your learning history to a MARUE WORKS server.

Third-party services used by the app, including Apple, RevenueCat, and Google AdMob, may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

N3 Bridge is intended for Japanese language learners.

N3 Bridge does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through its own account or server system.

If a third-party service is used, its own privacy policy and applicable age requirements apply.

8. Data Retention

Learning progress stored by N3 Bridge remains on your device unless the app data is removed or the application is deleted.

Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own policies.

Advertising-related data may be retained by Google according to its own policies.

9. Security

Because N3 Bridge stores learning progress locally on your device and does not require an account, the amount of personal information directly handled by MARUE WORKS is limited.

However, no digital service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when N3 Bridge features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be made available on the official privacy policy page.
