About Gadgetory

Gadgetory is a personal device tracker: it helps you keep a record of the gadgets you own, from the day you buy them to the day you let them go.

You can log purchases, repairs, battery replacements, accessories, cleaning or maintenance, and the eventual sale, gift, or disposal of a device, all on a timeline for each device. Gadgetory does not publish or share your records with anyone else.

Key features

  • Register devices with a photo
  • Track name, category, maker, model number, purchase date and price, store, warranty date, serial number, and notes
  • A timeline of repairs, battery replacements, and other history for each device
  • Cost-per-day and ownership-length display
  • Search by name, maker, or model number
  • "Let Go" and "Vault" to record devices you've sold, gifted, or disposed of, without deleting their history
  • Backup and restore
  • A one-time purchase, Gadgetory Unlimited, that removes the free plan's device limit
  • "Restore purchases"

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Gadgetory doesn't ask for sign-in, and there's no account to create.

How do I add a device?

Use "Add a device" from Home or your device list. Only a name is required — you can add a photo, category, purchase date, and price later.

Where are my records and photos stored?

On your device. Gadgetory does not automatically send your data to a server.

How many devices can I register on the free plan?

Up to 5. Devices you've already registered stay fully viewable and editable no matter how many you have.

What if I want to register a 6th device?

Purchasing Gadgetory Unlimited removes the device limit so you can register as many as you like. It's a one-time purchase — not a monthly or yearly subscription. The App Store shows the current price on the purchase screen.

Can I restore a purchase I already made?

Yes. Use "Restore purchases" on the Unlock Unlimited screen or in Settings. It checks your purchase history under the same Apple Account — use it after reinstalling the app or switching devices.

Does Gadgetory send notifications about warranty dates?

No, Gadgetory does not send push or local notifications. Warranty dates you enter are shown on the device's details and can be used to sort your device list.

What happens to a device I've sold or given away?

Use "Let Go" on a device's details to mark it as sold, gifted, or disposed of. This isn't deletion — its photos and history stay in the "Vault," and you can move it back to "Owned" if you change your mind.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Settings → "Backup & Restore" creates a single file containing your records and photos, then opens your device's own "Save" screen so you can choose where to keep it. Available save locations depend on your device.

Restoring works the same way: choose "Restore from backup" and pick a saved file. Restoring only adds records — it never overwrites what's already there, and there's no limit on how many devices can be restored.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Data stored on your device may be lost. Back up anything you want to keep before deleting the app.
